# 英文改写对照语料库（Before → After）

> 使用场景：修改英文文本时参考。每条示例按问题类型分类，展示“坏”和“好”的写法。
> 注意：这些示例来自四本书中的原例或改编。

## 1. 角色与动作（Character & Action）

### 名词化隐藏动作
- Before: `Our lack of data prevented evaluation of UN actions.`
- After: `Because we lacked data, we could not evaluate whether the UN had acted.`

- Before: `The committee's decision was to fund the project.`
- After: `The committee decided to fund the project.`

- Before: `A survey of this region was made in 1900.`
- After: `This region was surveyed in 1900.`

### 空洞动词 + 名词化
- Before: `The police conducted an investigation into the incident.`
- After: `The police investigated the incident.`

- Before: `The company made a decision to expand.`
- After: `The company decided to expand.`

## 2. 被动语态（Passive Voice）

### 无正当理由的被动
- Before: `The report was submitted by John.`
- After: `John submitted the report.`

- Before: `It was decided that the meeting would be postponed.`
- After: `We decided to postpone the meeting.`

### 正当被动（信息流需要）
- Before: `Scientists have raised some astonishing questions about black holes. The collapse of a dead star creates a black hole.`
- After: `Some astonishing questions about black holes have been raised by scientists. A black hole is created by the collapse of a dead star.`

## 3. 信息流（Topic & Stress）

### 旧信息未前置
- Before: `Some astonishing questions about black holes have been raised by scientists. The collapse of a dead star into a point no larger than a marble creates a black hole.`
- After: `Some astonishing questions about black holes have been raised by scientists. A black hole is created by the collapse of a dead star into a point no larger than a marble.`

### 强调位错误
- Before: `Humanity has hardly advanced in fortitude since that time, though it has advanced in many other ways.`
- After: `Humanity, since that time, has advanced in many other ways, but it has hardly advanced in fortitude.`

## 4. 简洁（Concision）

### 冗余表达
- Before: `used for fuel purposes`
- After: `used for fuel`

- Before: `the reason for the delay is because`
- After: `the reason for the delay is that` 或 `the delay occurred because`

- Before: `full and complete`
- After: `complete`

### 空洞短语
- Before: `it is important to note that`
- After: `note that`

- Before: `in the event that`
- After: `if`

## 5. 句子形状（Shape）

### 向左分支堆积
- Before: `the Lingling Brothers and Barnum & Bailey Circus`
- After: `the circus founded by the Lingling Brothers and Barnum & Bailey`

### 中心埋置
- Before: `The view that beating a third-rate Serbian military that for the third time in a decade is brutally targeting civilians is hardly worth the effort is not based on a lack of understanding.`
- After: `For the third time in a decade, a third-rate Serbian military is brutally targeting civilians, but beating it is hardly worth the effort.`

## 6. 平行结构（Parallelism）

- Before: `The authors suggest that different selection pressures affected cognitive ability, and intelligence differences are due to mutation-selection balance, while balancing selection can explain personality differences.`
- After: `The authors suggest that different selection pressures affected cognitive ability: mutation-selection balance can explain differences in cognitive ability, and balancing selection can explain differences in personality.`

## 7. 否定改肯定（Positive Form）

- Before: `He was not very often on time.`
- After: `He usually came late.`

- Before: `He did not think that studying Latin was much use.`
- After: `He thought the study of Latin useless.`

## 8. 用词（Word Choice）

- Before: `the question as to whether`
- After: `whether`

- Before: `owing to the fact that`
- After: `because`

- Before: `make an appearance`
- After: `appear`
